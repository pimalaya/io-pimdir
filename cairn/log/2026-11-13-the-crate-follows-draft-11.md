# The crate follows draft 11

spec/ is pimdir at d8800fb, storage-draft-11, sync-draft-11 and search-draft-11, the fourteen lines draft-10 parked for the standard's owner, decided on 2026-11-13 and landed there in one batch: the store schema gained one `CHECK` (`mail_summary.date`), one statement is new (`unconfirm_action`), one changed its text (`park_action`), and the vectors gained sync cases 155 to 158, three mail fixtures with two summary cases (73), two payload cases (75) and eight search cases (220), a ninth gaining its `complete`, no vector format moving. The copy was vendored before this pass, at the commit, and nothing under spec/ was edited here. The three tags do not exist yet: spec/REVISION names the commit carrying the drafts, and is renamed to the tagged one as ae8b231 was for draft-10. A store draft-10 wrote is refused by every profile and its index rebuilt, never migrated. tests/pending_vectors.txt is empty: every vendored case passes, none worked around.

## What followed

- **The draft names.** `sql::FORMAT_VERSION`, `sync::SYNC_VERSION`, `sql::search::FORMAT_VERSION` and `FORMAT_DRAFT`, README.md's conformance line and note, the spec's scenarios. The tests naming a later draft name draft-12 (tests/cli_owner.rs, tests/open_current.rs, tests/search.rs, tests/store_version.rs, src/version.rs), and draft-10 joins the refused and the rebuilt ones.
- **A message's date is held by the schema** (STORAGE §13). The derivation writes no other shape than the one the `CHECK` takes (`summary::mail::instant`, years 1900 to 9999 through `time::rfc3339`), and the whole suite ran on the new schema before any test was touched: no production path and no test's seeding tripped the constraint. What moved is what a caller can state: `PimdirSummary::stored` writes a date that is no instant at seconds precision as none, where it did so for an empty one alone, and the readers that took an empty date for none take such a date for none too (`summary::mail::usable_date`: `PimdirScope::contains`, the round's absences, the agreement of §9). `is_instant` moved from src/collection.rs to src/summary/time.rs, one function for a scope bound and a date.
- **The reserved prefix is the statements'** (STORAGE §13, §15.2). `PimdirStore::unconfirm_action` runs the canonical `unconfirm_action`: the statement writes the prefix, counts the attempt and answers the row's id, and writes nothing over an action the owner applies itself. The crate hands it the detail scrubbed, and reads the row only when the statement answered nothing, to tell a row that is gone (`false`) from such an action (`PimdirError::NotIntent`). `record_failure`, behind `fail_action` and every park of the drain, hands `park_action` its text scrubbed and adds no `failed: ` of its own. `codec::applied_by_owner`, the crate's own list of those actions, is deleted: the statement holds it. What each call records, counts, answers and refuses is what it was.
- **A move over a conflict** (SYNC §7; STORAGE §15.2 to §15.4). `PimdirMutate` refuses a `Move` meeting the item's conflict or its binding's before it reads its target, as `PimdirMutateError::Unresolved` with `resolves` `None` (`conflict-unresolved`), the refusal an ordinary `Edit` or `Remove` meets. The drain stages a queued `move` through the mutation, so it parks naming the diverging body, nothing written in either collection. A `Copy` is unchanged. This reverses what the payloads-and-identity follow recorded as open, and cairn/spec/mutate.md states the rule where it stated the question.
- **The overlay** (§15.4) folds nothing for such a `move`: the item stays listed and counted in the collection it would leave, and does not arrive in the one the row names (`get_item`, `count_items`, every page). A resolution queued before the move is read: the pending rows of the collection the item leaves are folded in order, for an item standing in a conflict alone, so `update` stating `resolves` then `move` shows the item moved, as the drain applies both.
- **Vectors 155 to 158.** 155 and 156 follow from the refusal. 157, an `Abandon` beside another source, passed as the crate stood: the drop marks the item deleted, the other source keeps its binding and projects the tombstone. 158 needed the rule below and a runner that seeds the `references` a case's store states, which no case stated before; tests/vectors_sync.rs seeds them through the canonical `add_reference`.
- **A key stands for one message** (STORAGE §11.1, §14.3). A body entering under a key another row of the account holds another body under, a message it does not agree with on `subject`, `date` and `sender`, records no stand-in and no reference (src/client/attachments.rs `another_message`). The agreement is `PimdirHeldObject::agrees`, hoisted out of the upgrade, which reads it with the size.
- **A multipart nothing closes** (Annex A.1). The derivation's `parts` and the streamed walk read what follows the last delimiter line as the last child, ended by the end of the message or by an outer delimiter, its size without the line break its entity ends with. The walk's bookkeeping of a child cut short (`child_start`, `invited_before`) is gone with the rule it served. The departure cairn/spec/summaries.md stated, the two walks bounding at neighbouring entities over 10,000 parts with one unended, is gone too: both count such a part.
- **An entity's fields from their first occurrence** (Annex A.1). The summaries' two walks read `Content-Type`, `Content-Disposition`, `Content-Transfer-Encoding` and `Content-ID` so already (`field`), which the new summary case confirms as the crate stood.

## Behaviour chosen where the text was open

- **A date of another shape is stored as none**, not refused. The text says no other text is taken; it does not say what an owner does with a summary a connector dated otherwise. Refusing fails the member's whole page on every run; none is what Annex A.1 writes for a `Date` it cannot read.
- **One disagreeing holder is enough.** With two rows holding one message and a third another, all under one `Message-ID`, a body of the first entering again under another form records nothing: §14.3 says "another row of the account holds another body under, a message it does not agree with", and nothing says whose the keys are then.
- **The row a body enters is no other row.** Its own earlier body, which `lookup_objects` answers until the write lands, is left out, or a draft edited down to its subject would record no part.
- **The overlay reads any conflict as parking a move.** A reader does not know which binding the owner runs a row through, so a move over an item no conflict of which touches the binding the drain picks is shown late, never wrongly, as an ordinary `update` already was.
- **`Unresolved` is reused** for the refused move, `resolves` `None`: no new variant, no new code, the message the one an ordinary edit over a conflict gets.
- **A delimiter line ending its entity starts a last part holding nothing**, as it does before a closing delimiter. It makes no mark and counts as an entity.
- **Only the last line break is the missing delimiter's.** An unclosed inner multipart whose last part is followed by two line breaks before the outer delimiter measures the first, as the standard's checker does through Python's parser. Read strictly by RFC 2046 it would measure neither.
- **A message cut between the `\r` and the `\n` ending its last part** keeps that `\r` in the part's size: Annex A.1's Lines has content measured keep every octet. The standard's checker, stripping `\r` as a line break, would not.
- **`EXTRACTOR` stays `io-pimdir 1`.** The extraction of a message stating a field twice changed, but no index written before survives: one of search-draft-10 is rebuilt.

## Problems found in the standard's batch

No vector contradicts its prose, and none is on tests/pending_vectors.txt. In the prose:

- **§8, a restore interrupted.** "A restore interrupted before that leaves a directory holding no pimdir.db" held from the removal of the replaced database on. Interrupted earlier, while it merged the bodies, the replaced pimdir.db still stood beside a local/ already replaced by the copy's. Settled in the review of this follow, both ways: the restore now merges the bodies, stages and raises the database under the local/ it found, removes the replaced database, and only then replaces what local/ holds, so it leaves the store it found until the new one is ready and no store after; and §8 says what each window leaves. tests/store_text.rs `a_restore_stopped_at_the_bodies_leaves_the_store_it_found` holds it, seen failing with local/ removed before the bodies (the state file read back as not found). `a_backup_taken_under_a_running_producer_is_consistent` assumed the producer thread had queued a row by each drain and failed 7 runs in 25 on a busy machine: it now waits for one, the product untouched.
- **§14.3, three rows.** The rule is stated for two messages; see "One disagreeing holder is enough".
- **Annex A.1, a lone `\r` ending an unclosed part.** Lines calls it the line break's, never the line's content, then has content measured keep every octet; the new sentence gives "the line break the entity ends with" to the missing delimiter. The two readings differ by one octet on a message cut there, and by one line break on the nested case above.
- **vectors/sync, `store.references`.** Vector 158 is the first whose store states references. The format allowed it; vectors/README.md may say a runner seeds them.
