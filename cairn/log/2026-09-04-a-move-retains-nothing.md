# A move retains nothing

A confirmed move dropped the source binding, and the store, retaining every item whose last binding goes, filed the message in the inbox trash beside its archive copy. The drop now asks whether another collection of the same account holds the identity live; when one does the row is retired and purged in one transaction, the holder pinning the body. The standard's STORAGE §11 says the same since today; store.md moved with it.
