# Annex A revision 3

Phase 3 of the-crate-follows-storage-draft-08. The summaries follow pimdir STORAGE Annex A at revision 3 (pimdir d671bbd, the identity-and-derivations, search-terms-bounds-and-versions, structure-security-and-wording and checks-run-every-statement-and-pin-every-vector entries): a message's header is read as A.1 states it, a zoned time resolves through the IANA database and at the offset before a gap, the walk of a message's parts is bounded by entities as well as depth, and an owner re-derives a store an earlier revision wrote. tests/summaries.rs passes all 53 cases, the nine phase-3 lines off tests/pending_vectors.txt.

## What landed

- **The header** (src/summary/mail.rs), each difference the pimdir entry's Followers list named: `lexemes` walks a structured value as RFC 5322 §3.2 does (nested comments, quoted-strings, a backslash quoting in either), `uncommented` reads each comment as one space. `instant` removes comments wherever they stand, skips the day of the week up to the first comma, takes five words with the whitespace around a colon not counting, the seconds optional, two-digit hours, minutes and seconds, a year of two digits or more read by RFC 5322 §4.3 then 1900 to 9999, `+hhmm`/`-hhmm` with `mm` at most 59 and five characters exactly, the named zones, one letter but `J`; a missing zone or a word after it, `24:00`, `30 Feb` are unparseable, a second of 60 reads as 59, an instant past 9999 is unparseable. `strip_angles` became the public `message_id`: comments skipped, the first `<` outside one to the next `>`, bracketless only without whitespace, `<`, `>`, `"` and with one `@`; `message_ids` reads a bracketless `In-Reply-To` word by word. `decode` scans every position for an encoded word of RFC 2047's syntax (a token charset, `B` or `Q`, printable ASCII but `?`), keeps a malformed one (`b_octets`: a character outside the alphabet, padding past two, a lone sextet; `q_octets`: an `=` without two hexadecimal digits) or one in an unknown charset as its literal text, removes the whitespace between two decoded words alone and trims spaces and tabs alone. `charset_decode`, now public, answers `Option<String>`: the six UTF-8 and seventeen windows-1252 WHATWG labels in every build (`iso-8859-1` and `us-ascii` among the latter, where they read as Latin-1), every other label through `encoding_rs::Encoding::for_label_no_replacement` under `charset-tables`, `None` otherwise. `addresses` splits through `lexemes`, `display_name` (public) reads comments as spaces and each quoted-string as its content wherever it stands. The header block ends at the first empty line at CRLF or bare LF (`split_headers` found `\n\n` and `\r\n\r\n` alone), a field's value is trimmed of spaces and tabs alone.
- **Decision 5**: `client` enables `charset-tables`; a build without it keeps a word in a label it has no table for as written, a deviation stated in summaries.md and the README. An RFC 2231 `filename*` in an unknown charset still reads as UTF-8 (src/summary/mail/attachments.rs), Annex A.7 stating no reading for one.
- **Decision 6** (src/summary/calendar.rs): `instant` resolves a `TZID` through the resource's `VTIMEZONE`, then `iana` (jiff's `TimeZone::get`, kept only when `iana_name` is the `TZID` byte for byte, since the bundled lookup ignores case), else floating; `iana` takes the `before` offset of jiff's `AmbiguousOffset` for a gap and a fold. `resolve` returns the offset before a transition whose gap or fold holds the wall time, else the one offset in effect at the instant it names. `transitions` reads, per observance, its `DTSTART`, its last onset before three days around the wall time and every one within them; `ZoneRule` follows a fixed-step rule (`SECONDLY` to `WEEKLY`, `INTERVAL`, `COUNT`, `UNTIL`) in closed form and a `YEARLY` one by `BYMONTH` and `BYDAY` (an ordinal, or a `BYMONTHDAY` list) year by year, each for at most 1,000,000 periods (`ZONE_PERIODS_MAX`); before, a yearly rule ignored its `UNTIL` and `COUNT` and any other rule gave its `DTSTART` alone, which is why the hostile-zone vector already passed. jiff 0.2 (`alloc`, `tzdb-bundle-always`) is a direct dependency of the core, resolving to the 0.2.38 ical-rs's `tzdb` already locked, so src/search/occurrence.rs and the derivation read one database version and occurrence.rs needed no change.
- **The walk** (Annex A.1, STORAGE §14.3): `MIME_ENTITIES_MAX` 10,000 beside `MIME_DEPTH_MAX` 64. The derivation's `has_attachment` and `invitation` count the entities they read in document order, the message's own first; `PimdirAttachmentWalk` counts each part as it begins and skips one past the bound, its levels' delimiters still read so a part they never end is still none; the extraction (src/search/extract.rs) marks the parts read by a depth-first pass over mail-parser's tree (`Walk::over`) and gathers text from those alone, which also reads a text part at depth 64 the old `depth >= MIME_DEPTH_MAX` check skipped. `ATTACHMENTS_MAX` was already 1,000. Both walks now take a line as a delimiter only when it is the boundary, `--` when it closes, then spaces and tabs (`delimits`, RFC 2046 §5.1.1): vector "the walk's depth" nests `d1` around `d10`, and `--d10` closed the level of `d1`, misnumbering every part under it.
- **A.2 to A.5**: a card's `KIND` and `ORG`, an event's organizer and attendees and a journal were derived already; the three new summaries cases (card-kind.vcf, journal-basic.ics, event-people.ics) needed only the IANA fallback, event-people's sort key.
- **`DERIVATION` 3** (src/client/schema.rs). `PimdirStore::rederive`, run by `open` and `open_current` after the open contract, pages `list_held_mail` then `list_held_tasks` 256 keys at a time when the store records an earlier revision: each body read through `PimdirBlobs::get` outside the transaction (one absent or failing its name derives nothing, the failing one quarantined), derived by `summary::derive` under the collection kind, then written in one immediate transaction per page by `write::rederive_item`: `read_hub` for the page's keys gives the held summary and sort key, `write_summary` restates the summary, addresses and lists when they moved, `set_sort_key` the key, `link_references` the automatic references, `stamp_item` a stamp, each only when something moved; `set_derivation` records 3 in the last page's transaction. Link ids are never re-derived. `record_derivation` still records 3 over a store at a later revision with the first derived write.

## Departures

- The streamed walk counts a part as it begins and the derivation's walk the parts a delimiter ends, so in a message past 10,000 entities that also leaves a part unended the two may bound at neighbouring entities; a delimiter line longer than `LINE_HEAD` is read on its first 1,024 octets by the streamed walk alone. Both are stated in summaries.md.
- A `VTIMEZONE` observance's `RRULE` of another shape than the two followed, and its `RDATE` list, contribute nothing beyond its `DTSTART`: Annex A.3 names no shape a writer must follow, and the sort key is exempt from byte identity (§13).
- No event is paged by the re-derivation, as pimdir's entry states: revision 3 moved a zoned event sort key only where a transition skips the time or no `VTIMEZONE` names the zone, restated when the item is next derived.

## Notes for pimdir

Not disagreements with the annex, which this phase implements as written, but places checks/vectors.py's second reading is looser than the prose:

- `header_fields` splits the block at `\r?\n\r?\n`, so a body opening on an empty line keeps reading fields after it, where A.1 ends the block at the first empty line; no vector opens on one.
- `mail_date` matches the day, the year and the clock with Python's `\d`, which takes any Unicode digit and `int()` reads; A.1 says digits, read here as ASCII.
- A.1 is silent on whether a part no delimiter ends is an entity the walk read, and A.3 on which `RRULE` shapes and whether `RDATE` an observance contributes; a hostile message or zone can tell two conforming writers apart there.
