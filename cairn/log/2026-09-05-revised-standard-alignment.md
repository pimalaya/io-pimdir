# The engine and the store on the audited standard

The standard's algorithm audit landed nine changes on 2026-09-05 and the spec was re-vendored byte for byte: five statements new (`delete_collection`, `rename_queue_targets`, `list_pending_actions`, `pin_object`, `list_conflicted_items`), four gone (`bump_next_change`, `delete_items`, `list_object_hashes`, `list_queued_collections`), three triggers, `stamp_item` requesting a stamp, `load_change_cursor` answering the last stamp drawn, `held_elsewhere` bound to the body, the trash over every deleted row, `lookup_objects` joining sizes. One owner statement the std client needed, `collection_sources`, was written here and upstreamed the same day (spec-fidelity).

The std client: the schema check reads tables and triggers and `Stale` names what is missing; `enqueue` pins before the row lands; the drain is store-wide in append order, a busy or I/O failure bumping attempts without stopping the pass and `MAX_ATTEMPTS` parking, each action run as a source binding its item or syncing its collection rather than the handle draining, a move into an undeclared kind parking, an `add` sitting under the provisional handle; `rename_collection` retargets the queued moves and copies; `delete_collection` cascades and recomputes; `collect_garbage` refuses while a verb of this process runs and recomputes before it deletes; a revive keeps a retained body a `Meta` fetch carries none of; `list_item_conflicts`, `list_pending_actions`, `collection_sources` join the read surface and `PimdirRetention::at` is optional (store, cli).

The engine: `KeepBoth` and the delete policy are gone, a refused delete held beside another source of the collection and reverted alone, the consumer stating it through `beside_other_sources`; a vanished local edit is re-staged under its provisional handle instead of dropped; a create waits while the collection holds a probe; an item-level conflict projects `Conflict` on every binding with no revision, derives nothing, and an `Edit` or a `Remove` settles it, the tombstone clearing it in the hub; a tombstone holding a staged edit over a remote edit follows the policy (sync). The hub reads the three facts of a divergence against the base the binding held, judges mutability across every binding, lets an immutable kind's base adopt the shared body, offers its copies under the provisional handle, and keeps a rebuilt binding's base aside for the upsert that rebinds it (hub). Every create sits under `U+0001` then the link id, a `Remove` settles a conflict against the recorded revision, and a copy of a placement with neither body nor based binding is `Undeliverable` (mutate). A `Full` fetch moves the base, or marks a conflict over a staged edit the remote moved past; a diverging body equal to the placement's own clears the conflict; a mutable member restating its hint is keyed afresh; a link on a `Message-ID` needs the summary's size to agree, `LookupObject` carrying `PimdirObject`s (upgrade). A rebuild lists every `Rekeyed` drop before every upsert, pairs the copies of one hint by base revision then body, and carries an item-level conflict with no revision, which is what had one source's pushes held for ever after a bump while the others settled the item (rekey, seam).

Thirty-two vectors reproduce, and every property suite converges again.
