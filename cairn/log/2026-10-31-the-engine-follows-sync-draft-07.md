# The engine follows sync-draft-07

Phase 5 of the-crate-follows-storage-draft-08, after pimdir's 2026-10-31-sync-rules-and-the-write-contract (pimdir d671bbd), the "One table assigns a key; held is any row" and "A second loser through one binding is kept" rules of 2026-10-31-identity-and-derivations, the "Terms" rule of 2026-10-31-structure-security-and-wording and the "Strict vector formats" rule of 2026-10-31-checks-run-every-statement-and-pin-every-vector. The ten phase-5 sync vectors (15, 106 to 110, 114 to 117) pass and left tests/pending_vectors.txt; 04, 16, 47, 61, 104, 105 and 111 to 113 still pass.

## What landed

- **The connector's fields** (src/remote.rs). `PimdirRemoteSnapshot` gains `narrowed: bool` and `enumerated: Option<Vec<PimdirHandle>>`, documented per protocol: IMAP narrowing by `SENTSINCE` hands `UID SEARCH ALL`, JMAP narrowing by `after` an `Email/query` by the mailbox alone, Graph narrowing by `receivedDateTime` the folder's ids, Gmail narrowing by `after:` the label's ids where it lists them. The four constructors state neither; the struct literals in src/rekey.rs, src/sync.rs and tests/engine/common.rs say so; the CLI builds none.
- **The narrowed absence** (SYNC §4, §5; STORAGE §10). A round's last page loads `PimdirLoadSelection::LastPage { narrowed, enumerated }` (a head's and a delta's statements are dropped, proving no absence); the store answers `unstamped` from `list_unstamped_bindings` bound with `:narrowed`, `:enumerated` and the enumeration in the `handles` slot (src/client/write.rs `unstamped_bindings`), and `All` answers none. A narrowed round its own last page would open is opened first in a write of its own (`OpenRound`, the new `State::Opening`), since only the store stamps `round_started_at`, which the rule compares a `date` with; its absences are then read as a resumed round's. A round opened and closed by one unnarrowed page keeps the in-memory rule (`RoundPlan::finds_absent`).
- **The empty-listing park** (SYNC §5, §8). `PimdirParked::guard(absent, bound, named, accepted)` parks on any absence when the listing named no member. A round named one when a page or a head of this run listed one (`PimdirSync::named`, reset when the round restarts under a new id) or when an earlier run's page stamped a binding, read from the store as a based binding in the round's scope, whose absence its listing could prove, that `unstamped` lacks (`PimdirSync::stamped`, for a round the run resumed: the store reads no statement answering `bindings.round`). A rekey names one when any page listed a member.
- **The mutable revive at `Meta`** (STORAGE §11.1, SYNC §6). `revive_item` (src/client/write.rs), meeting a retained body with an incoming placement of a kind other than `message/rfc822` carrying none, revives the row at `Meta` holding no body and sets the retained body aside (`PimdirOverruled`, its summary the retained row's through the new `held_summary`, keyed by `keep_overruled` over the reviving binding's handle), reported `Overruled` after the member's `Added`. A message keeps the retained body as its base as before, the agreement below having checked its size.
- **The mail agreement against every held row** (STORAGE §9, §14; SYNC §6, §10). `mint_other_messages` (src/client/write.rs), on a mail collection, checks every upsert naming a handle this source does not bind against the row its load read under the key, a live item other sources or none bind or a tombstone (`summary::same_message`), and the retained row under it (the new `retained_summary`, `summary::same_retained_message`, on size too where both state one); a disagreeing one is minted over its handle, minted again over a held key, and a create staged under the key's provisional handle moves to the minted key's (`Add`, `Copy`, `Move`). A row this source binds is the engine's to key, a pending create's landing included (`take_pending_create`'s `same_message`).
- **No policy against an unread body** (SYNC §5, §6, §9). `PreferLocal` meeting a member that carries no body records the divergence as `Manual` does (`resolve_conflict`), pushing nothing. The item's divergence recorded without its own body is settled when the upgrade fills it: the hub's fill (src/hub.rs `reconcile_content`) settles a `prefer-` item conflict, `prefer-incoming` adopting the diverging body (its summary derived from its bytes by the write, `PimdirHub::unsummarized`) and `prefer-existing` keeping the filled one, the body not adopted kept over the filling binding's handle (`PimdirLoserSide::Incoming` or `Diverging`), `manual` leaving it standing. The upgrade (src/upgrade.rs `settle`) settles a binding's divergence whose body it fetches by the source's policy, the new `PimdirUpgradeOptions` (`conflict`, `push_content`, `From<&PimdirSyncOptions>`; `PimdirUpgrade::options`, a parameter of `PimdirSourceStore::upgrade`): `Manual`, and `PreferLocal` without content pushes, record it in `conflict_object`; `PreferLocal` writes the divergence, an `Overrule` and the placement with its base on the fetched body at its revision, the conflict cleared; `PreferRemote` clears the binding's conflict first, then adopts the body and base, so the hub keeps the local edit as a `PreferRemote` pull's. A landing fetch carrying another body than the staged one settles the same way.
- **`PreferLocal`'s keep** (SYNC §5, §9). `keep_observed` (src/sync.rs) upserts the binding conflicted with the member's body as its `conflict_object`, writes an `Overrule`, then upserts the placement with its base on that body at the observed revision, the conflict cleared; the flag axis merges on that placement (`ContentOutcome::Push { placement, change }`), and `flush` hands the batch over before any push chunk, which then pushes the local body gated on that revision. `refuse_unread_overrule` reads the binding as the batch left it before the `Overrule`, so a keep recording the body it read is never refused.
- **Decision 7**: `PimdirLoadScope` is `PimdirLoadSelection`, `PimdirYield::WantsLoad`'s `scope` is `selection`, and `PimdirSourceStore::load`'s and `PimdirMutation::scope`'s (now `selection`) follow, in src/, tests/, cairn/spec/seam.md and store.md, and in cairn/changes/a-delta-loads-what-it-names (its text references only).

## Departures

- The narrowing reaches the store as a selection, `LastPage`, rather than a field of every load: only a round's last page reads absences, and `All` no longer answers them (a rekey's and a delta's loads never used them).
- A narrowed round opened by its own last page takes one more write (`OpenRound` alone) before its load; a run failing between the two leaves an open round holding no cursor, which the next run lists from its first page, as a parked one.
- Whether an earlier run's page named a member is derived from `unstamped`, no statement answering `bindings.round`: a stamped binding outside the round's scope or the set its listing could prove is not seen, and the round parks, the conservative reading.
- `PreferLocal`'s keep and the upgrade's are modelled as the overruling `Edit` they cite (SYNC §5: "as a person's `Edit` adopts a conflict"): the divergence recorded, an `Overrule`, the adopting upsert, in one batch. The write's own loser rule keeps `PreferRemote`'s dropped edit in the upgrade once the binding's conflict is cleared by a first upsert of the batch.
- The mutable revive reads the kind as the collection's (`message/rfc822` immutable, any other mutable) and the reviving binding as the item's one binding, a batch being one source's.
- The write's agreement runs on mail collections alone (another kind agrees with anything, STORAGE §9) and on a handle this source does not bind.
- Unchanged, as asked: S10 (a local `Edit` over a binding whose base names a revision and no body still pushes gated on it) and the absence-propagation guard. Also unchanged: an `Add` over a live item another source alone binds is not refused, the `Links` load answering this source's holders (STORAGE §14) and the write joining it; pimdir's table says refused.

## Notes for pimdir

- `list_unstamped_bindings` compares `s.date < r.round_started_at` as text: a summary `date` at fixed width with no fraction (`…:00Z`) against `strftime('%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%fZ')` (`…:00.123Z`) reads the same second as later (`Z` sorts after `.`), so a member dated within the second the round opened is not provable by its date. Conservative and harmless, but the two formats differ.
- checks/vectors.py's absence leaves a tombstone of the run's source out of `absent` (`held`), where SYNC §5 counts "every based binding of this source the round did not stamp"; this engine counts it, so an empty listing finding only a tombstone absent parks. No vector holds the case.
- The "named" reading needs the store's `bindings.round`; a statement answering whether any binding of the source carries the open round's id would let an engine read it exactly.
