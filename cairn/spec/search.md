# Search

The reference implementation of pimdir SEARCH.md (`search-draft-11`, an indexer and a query client of the canonical shape), experimental: its API and index build may change in a minor release: index.db beside the store, its refresh from the change feed, extraction and the query language. It ships behind the `search` feature, which `cli` enables and the default leaves out, so a consumer that does not search carries none of it. `search` alone is I/O-free (src/search/: the query language, the folds, extraction, chunks, occurrences, tags, threads and the refresh coroutine); `search` with `client` adds `PimdirIndex`, index.db over rusqlite (src/client/search.rs). Milestones M1 to M4 of pimdir's search-milestones: every kind's extraction, every field, occurrences within the horizon, threads, relative dates, tagging and the thread verb; and search-draft-08's bounds, versions, retries and prune.

### Requirement: The index is derived and rebuilt, never migrated
`PimdirIndex::open` SHALL open index.db as the main database with pimdir.db attached read-only as `store`, and SHALL read what it holds before writing anything (SEARCH §2, `PimdirIndexHeld` in src/client/search.rs). It SHALL refuse as `PimdirError::IndexVersion` (`index-version`, naming the index's version and shape) an index.db holding a schema and no `index_meta`, one whose `shape` is not `canonical`, and one whose `format_version` lies above `search-draft-10` or names no draft, never rebuilding, writing nor deleting it: the journal mode is set only once the index is found its own or built. It SHALL rebuild one of its shape whose `format_version` lies below its own or which lacks the column, one at another schema version, tokenizer or schema text (every table, index and trigger migrations/search/0001_init.sql creates, compared by its `sqlite_schema` text with `--` comments removed and whitespace collapsed, the shadow tables FTS5 creates and any other table left out), one built from another store or another copy of it (`index_meta.store_id` and `store_incarnation` against `load_change_cursor`'s), and one whose `store_change` lies above the store's last stamp. The schema text is compared before `load_index_meta` is run, which names columns an index of an earlier text lacks: one written under the first batches of draft-10, holding neither `index_meta.extraction` nor `extractor` nor the index `object_not_ok`, is rebuilt on that ground. `refresh` SHALL make the same check under index.lock and rebuild an index a store was restored under. Versions are ordered by their draft number below every release (`sql::search::FORMAT_DRAFT`). `PimdirIndex::open_query` SHALL refuse an index of another shape or version as `IndexVersion` and one this build would otherwise rebuild as `PimdirError::IndexOutdated`, but for the extraction's revisions (below). It SHALL refuse a SQLite without FTS5 or older than 3.43 (`PimdirError::Fts5`) rather than build a partial index.

#### Scenario: An index of another schema text
- GIVEN an index.db of this version whose `placement_by_link` index was dropped
- WHEN `open_query` opens it, THEN it answers `IndexOutdated`; WHEN `open` opens it, THEN it rebuilds it and the next `open_query` opens it (tests/search.rs `an_index_of_another_schema_text_is_rebuilt_never_queried`)

#### Scenario: Another shape, a later version, no index_meta
- GIVEN an index.db whose `shape` is `fts4`, whose `format_version` is `search-draft-12` or `search-v1`, or holding no `index_meta`
- WHEN `open` or `open_query` opens it, THEN each answers `IndexVersion` naming what it found, and index.db is byte for byte what it was, no WAL frame written (tests/search.rs `an_index_of_another_shape_or_a_later_version_is_refused_and_left_as_it_is`)

#### Scenario: An earlier version
- GIVEN an index.db at `search-draft-07`, `search-draft-09` or `search-draft-10`, or lacking `format_version`
- WHEN `open_query` opens it, THEN it answers `IndexVersion`; WHEN `open` opens it, THEN it is rebuilt at `search-draft-11` (tests/search.rs `an_index_of_an_earlier_version_is_rebuilt_never_queried`)

#### Scenario: Another store, another copy, a cursor ahead
- GIVEN an index.db naming another `store_id` or `store_incarnation`, or a `store_change` above the store's last stamp, or a store whose incarnation `raise_incarnation` raised under an open index
- WHEN `open_query` opens it, THEN it answers `IndexOutdated`; WHEN `open` opens it or `refresh` folds into it, THEN it is rebuilt from the store as it stands (tests/search.rs `an_index_built_from_another_store_or_copy_is_rebuilt`)

### Requirement: An index records the revisions of its extraction
The crate SHALL state the revision of SEARCH §6 it extracts and expands under, `search::extract::EXTRACTION` (1), and its own revision of what §6 leaves to an indexer, `search::extract::EXTRACTOR` (`io-pimdir 1`), an opaque text bumped in the change that alters what the extraction of any body yields: the reduction of `text/html`, the charsets decoded, what a field holds, or a bump of mime-rs, vcard-rs, ical-rs or encoding_rs reading a body otherwise. `init_index_meta` SHALL record both (SEARCH §2). `PimdirIndex::open`, and `refresh` under index.lock, SHALL rebuild an index recording another of either, the texts compared byte for byte (`PimdirIndexHeld::Extracted`): the rebuild, which SEARCH §2 allows in place of extracting every held body again, leaves what a rebuild writes by construction, and its `init_index_meta` records the crate's own, so the crate runs no `set_extraction`, whose constant (`sql::search::SET_EXTRACTION`) stands for a consumer extracting again in place. `PimdirIndex::open_query` SHALL answer from such an index and never refuse it: the hits are the index's, and a snippet is cut from the chunks of this build's own extraction (`PimdirExtract::chunks`) rather than the stored offsets, which index another extraction's text (SEARCH §6). The refresh coroutine reads neither revision: a consumer of another shape compares the two constants with what its index records before it runs one, and rebuilds or extracts again itself, as it judges the version and the tokenizer.

#### Scenario: An index another extractor wrote
- GIVEN an index of three bodies and a calendar item, its `index_meta.extractor` then restated `another 7` (`set_extraction`), or its `extraction` 2
- WHEN `open_query` opens it, THEN it answers its hits and a snippet; WHEN `open` opens it or `refresh` folds into it, THEN the index is rebuilt, every body indexed and the calendar item expanded again, and it records `io-pimdir 1` under revision 1 (tests/search.rs `an_index_under_another_extraction_is_rebuilt_and_every_body_indexed_again`, which rebuilds nothing without the comparison)

#### Scenario: An index lacking a column the statements read
- GIVEN an index.db naming this draft whose `index_meta` lacks `extractor`
- WHEN `open_query` opens it, THEN it answers `IndexOutdated`; WHEN `open` opens it, THEN it is rebuilt (tests/search.rs `an_index_of_the_earlier_draft_10_text_is_rebuilt`, which failed on "no such column" with `load_index_meta` read first)

### Requirement: The index statements are bound as re-signed
The index SHALL bind the search statements as search-draft-11 signs them (SEARCH §2, §3): `init_index_meta` writing the format version, the shape, the revisions of the extraction and the store's id and incarnation off the attached store, `load_index_meta` read by its fourteen columns (`version`, `tokenizer`, `store_change`, `store_purges`, `horizon_start`, `horizon_end`, `tzdb_version`, `store_id`, `store_incarnation`, `format_version`, `shape`, `floating_zone`, `extraction`, `extractor`), `coverage` by its five (`kind`, `bodiless`, `missing`, `unparseable`, `encrypted`), `set_index_cursor` with `:store_change` and `:store_purges` alone, `items_to_reexpand` with `:floating_zone` bound to the zone the refresh states, `placements_gone` read by its three columns, the link id naming the thread to prune, `coverage` and `cut_before` with the `collections` slot, and `hit` with `:seq` and `:account`, read by its nine columns (`collection`, `link_id`, `flags`, `object_hash`, `sort_key`, `level`, `kind`, `kind_value`, `title`): every live placement of a hit in collection order, the first naming its kind (the `kind:` value, else the collection's media type for a calendar item holding no summary row) and its title. The crate keeps no text of its own for it. Every statement of SEARCH §3's table is run but `set_extraction`, which the rebuild makes needless (above). The order by relevance is the crate's own statement over the rank `match_objects` and `match_summaries` answer, the whole hit set in one statement, bodies and bodiless placements' summaries ranked together (SEARCH §8.2).

#### Scenario: A roll over a recurring journal
- GIVEN an index holding a journal with a yearly rule, expanded under a horizon
- WHEN a refresh rolls the horizon, THEN `items_to_reexpand` names the journal, whose summary does not say whether it recurs, and its occurrences are those of the new window; a refresh under the same horizon expands none (tests/search.rs `a_rolled_horizon_re_expands_a_recurring_journal`, which fails on the draft-09 text)

#### Scenario: An empty collection deleted
- GIVEN an index at rest and an empty collection of its store
- WHEN the collection is deleted, THEN the store draws no stamp and counts one purge, and the next refresh reconciles its keys, removing nothing (tests/search.rs `an_empty_collection_s_delete_is_reconciled`)

### Requirement: A refresh folds the feed in bounded, resumable batches
`PimdirIndex::refresh` SHALL take index.lock without waiting, answering `busy` when another indexer holds it. It SHALL fold `load_changed_items` in transactions of a bounded number of rows, each one run of `PimdirIndexRefresh` answered through the canonical statements (src/client/search/refresh.rs), each full batch recording through `set_index_cursor` its last row's stamp with the purge count the last completed pass recorded, so a pass cut short resumes where it stopped. The first run of a pass SHALL first fold again the placements `missing` records (`PimdirIndexRefresh::missing`). The transaction exhausting the feed SHALL reconcile keys when the store's purge count moved from `index_meta.store_purges`, and record the cursor read before the pass as `store_change` with the purge count read beside it. A deadline SHALL be checked between batches. A handle opened by `open_query` SHALL refuse to refresh (`PimdirError::IndexQueryOnly`).

### Requirement: One-shot answers refresh first and say what is behind
`search` and `count` SHALL refresh up to the store's head within `--refresh-timeout` before answering (`0s` skips it), and SHALL count what stays unfolded as `coverage.local.unindexed`; every answer SHALL say in `coverage.local.behind` (`behind` in JSON) what the canonical `behind` answers: the change stamps the store drew above the index cursor, store-wide. A session that keeps the index open follows the feed instead.

### Requirement: Mail is extracted through mime-rs, chunked as SEARCH §6 says
Mail extraction SHALL go through mime-rs (`parser`, `base64`, `encoding`): its MIME tree walked under `MIME_DEPTH_MAX` and `MIME_ENTITIES_MAX`, the parts the summaries' walk reads (a text part at depth 64 included, none after the 10,000th entity, the parse itself bounding no entity count so a multipart holding the 10,000th is still split), transfer encodings, charsets, RFC 2047 and 2231, `encrypted` and `unparseable` statuses, the attachment field from the crate's own stand-in walk. A header field the extraction reads (`Subject`, the address fields, `In-Reply-To` and `References`) SHALL be read from its first occurrence in document order, its name matched as STORAGE Annex A.1 matches one, whitespace before the colon included, so the index holds the subject and the people the summary row states (SEARCH §6; `extract::first`; tests/search_pure.rs `the_extraction_reads_mail_as_the_summary_does`, `the_extraction_and_the_summary_read_one_field`, the second on the standard's fixture mail-repeated-fields.eml). Until draft-10's second batch it read the last. The extraction SHALL walk every part the summaries' walk reads, the last child of a multipart no closing delimiter ends among them, and SHALL read an entity's own fields, `Content-Type`, `Content-Disposition` and `Content-Transfer-Encoding`, from their first occurrence, as Annex A.1 reads them (SEARCH §6, search-draft-11). mime-rs 0.1 reads the three from their last occurrence, in the accessors of an entity and in the parse deciding whether an entity is a multipart, a message or a leaf, and its options state no other reading, so a message one of whose entities states one twice (`extract::restates`, read off the tree mime-rs parsed, the names matched as the summaries match one) is parsed again from `summary::mail::first_occurrences`: the message with the later occurrences cut out of the header block of each entity the summaries' walk reads, every other octet as it was, which mime-rs then reads as Annex A.1 reads the original ("The walkers of a message agree", below). A message stating none twice is parsed once, as before. A text part disposed with any type but `inline` is an attachment, as Annex A.1's mark reads it, and gives no body text; until then `attachment` alone was. A text part SHALL be transcoded from its `charset`, UTF-8 without one and windows-1252 under a US-ASCII label (as the WHATWG Encoding Standard reads it), no byte order mark sniffed and `format=flowed` text kept as written; a part of a `multipart/digest` stating no type is a `message/rfc822` and gives no text (RFC 2046 §5.1.5). Within an alternative the first child that is or holds a `text/plain` part SHALL be read, else the last; `text/html` SHALL be reduced to text by src/search/html.rs, the rules of mail-parser 0.11's `html_to_text` and its named character references, which mime-rs does not offer yet, but for one: a tag SHALL separate the text on its two sides, as whitespace does, unless it is the tag of an inline element, so `<td>Order number</td><td>12345</td>` reads `Order number 12345` and `H<sub>2</sub>O` stays one token. The inline elements (`INLINE`) are `a`, `abbr`, `acronym`, `b`, `bdi`, `bdo`, `big`, `cite`, `code`, `data`, `del`, `dfn`, `em`, `font`, `i`, `ins`, `kbd`, `mark`, `nobr`, `q`, `rp`, `rt`, `ruby`, `s`, `samp`, `small`, `span`, `strike`, `strong`, `sub`, `sup`, `time`, `tt`, `u`, `var` and `wbr`, compared by the tag's first word whatever its case and its attributes; every other tag separates, `br`, `img`, the form controls and a name HTML does not define included, and a comment separates nothing. A separator never opens the text nor a line, and a token ended by a tag takes the space owed before it by the same rule as one ended by whitespace (src/search/html.rs `a_tag_separates_unless_inline`; `tags_go_and_line_breaks_stay` read `HelloWorld` for two `div`s before). The content of the `head`, `style`, `script` and `template` elements SHALL be skipped, each told by its tag's first word whatever its case and its attributes, so `<style type="text/css">` gives no text (`a_hidden_element_is_skipped_whatever_its_attributes`; told only as a one-word tag before, the CSS of a styled message was indexed). Both are what SEARCH §6 says a reduction should do; the reduction stays the crate's own, outside the hit-set guarantee, and a change to it bumps `EXTRACTOR`, so every index is extracted again (above). A body whose first block holds no header field SHALL read as the text after its first line of blanks, and as `unparseable` when no such line ends that block. The `body` and `note` fields SHALL be cut into chunks by `search::chunk::chunks` on SEARCH §6's rule. Contacts and calendar items SHALL be extracted through vcard-rs and ical-rs on SEARCH §6's table, a `TEL` as written and as its digits, every event, task and journal of a resource, overrides included; an item falls back to its summary only while bodiless or unparseable, and a file always reads its summary.

### Requirement: Calendar time is expanded through ical-rs within a rolling horizon
Occurrences SHALL be expanded by ical-rs's `IcalRecurTimeline` with zones from `ical::tzdb::zones`, so the resource's own `VTIMEZONE` wins over the bundled database, within a horizon of one year back and two forward around the refresh's `now`, under the bounds of SEARCH §7 (below). A floating time, a date and a `TZID` neither names SHALL be read in the zone the refresh states (below). An item SHALL be re-expanded when its body changes, when the horizon has rolled by a day or more, when `ical::tzdb::version()` differs from the recorded one, which `set_tzdb_version` then records, when the floating zone differs from the recorded one, and, the index being rebuilt, when a revision of the extraction does (above). A resource holding overrides and no master yields its overrides. `date:` and `when:` SHALL compare a bodied calendar item's occurrences, and coverage SHALL report `horizonLeft` when a range leaves the horizon with a calendar collection in scope.

### Requirement: A query is parsed whole or refused with SEARCH §8.4's object
`PimdirQuery::parse` SHALL accept exactly SEARCH §8.3's grammar within its limits (below) and refuse anything else with a `PimdirQueryError` carrying §8.4's code and span, never reading an unknown field as text. A relative amount holds at most six digits (`RELATIVE_DIGITS_MAX`) and a change stamp at most eighteen (`STAMP_DIGITS_MAX`), a longer one refused as `invalid-date` and `invalid-value` (SEARCH §8.3; the vector cases `date:1000000d` and `changed:1000000000000000000`). A parenthesised group SHALL parse to `PimdirQuery::Group`, matching as its operand does, so `PimdirQuery::conjuncts` reads through one that is itself a conjunct of the whole query and through no other (SEARCH §8.2). The value of a role field (`from:`, `to:`, `cc:`, `bcc:`, `attendee:`, `organizer:`, `email:`) or of `with:` holding `@` is an address, which takes no wildcard; in any other text field (`subject:`, `title:`, `summary:`, `body:`, `person:`, `attachment:`, `mimetype:`, `location:`) an `@` is a character as another, so `subject:a@b` is a text term and `subject:ledger@exa*` a prefix (SEARCH §8.3, §8.5, search-draft-11; src/search/query.rs `an_at_sign_is_an_address_in_a_role_field_alone`, which fails with every text field's `@` read as an address). The crate already compiled such a value as a text term; only the wildcard was refused. U+0000 in a text term SHALL compile to a space (SEARCH §8.5; src/client/search/compile.rs `a_nul_in_a_text_term_is_written_as_a_space`), FTS5 reading no expression past one. On `unknown-field` its `expected` SHALL be empty, `fields` carrying the list, and a name that lowercases to a field SHALL be named in the message. A statement failing on a query the grammar accepted SHALL answer `PimdirError::Unanswerable`, never a raw SQL error. The AST SHALL be public, so a consumer can compile its own filter into it.

### Requirement: Every answer carries its coverage
A search answer SHALL carry a `PimdirSearchCoverage` over the query's collections (SEARCH §8.2): every collection of the store less those an `account:`, `collection:`, `folder:` or `kind:` term rules out, and only a term standing as a conjunct of the whole query rules any out (`PimdirQuery::conjuncts`: the query itself when it is one term, else the terms its top-level `and` joins, in reading order). A group is read through (search-draft-11): one that is itself such a conjunct lends the query its own conjuncts, at any depth, so `(collection:a) x`, `((collection:a) (x))` and `collection:a x`, which answer one hit set, have one scope and one coverage. A term negated, beside an `or`, or inside a group that is negated or stands beside an `or`, rules none out. The date range a coverage reads its temporal axis and its cut over is narrowed by the same conjuncts. It holds the local axis (`local.kinds`, per kind the four counts the canonical `coverage` answers, `PimdirSearchUnread`: `bodiless` from the store's `items_bodiless`, `missing`, `unparseable` and `encrypted` from the index, a body filed twice counted once per placement, a kind counting nothing left out and a file never counted; `local.reads`, what the query's terms read; the items not yet folded and the lag), the temporal axis (`Full`, or `Bounded` by the collections' coverage rows), the remote axis (`LocalOnly` unless a consumer merged a server answer), whether the range left the horizon, for a query bounded by date the calendar items its collections hold whose occurrences a bound cut before the range's end (`cut`, `cut_before`, its end `NULL` for a range open there), and `zone_differs`: whether the zone the query's clock states, `UTC` for none, is another name than `index_meta.floating_zone`, for a query holding a date term whose collections hold a calendar one (SEARCH §8.1). The roll-up keeps the worst of each axis. `PimdirReader::search_coverage` SHALL fill the same type for the store's `LIKE` reads, which stay for consumers built without the feature: the `bodiless` count alone, the store knowing no extraction, under what a text term reads (`PimdirSearchReads::text`), those reads searching a text in summaries, so an item holding no body counts against them as it did.

### Requirement: One call says whether an answer is complete
`PimdirSearchCoverage::is_complete` SHALL answer SEARCH §8.2's complete answer, the lag included, so a consumer never adds `behind == 0` itself: `local.behind` and `local.unindexed` are zero, the temporal axis is `Full`, no range left the horizon, no item was cut, no server answer was left partial, no kind counts a `bodiless` item, a `missing` or an `unparseable` placement while a term reads a body of that kind, mail counts no `bodiless` item while the query holds `has:attachment`, and no kind counts an `encrypted` placement while a text term is matched in the `body` or the `attachment` field. A zone that differs leaves the answer complete, and so does a mailbox listed and not yet fetched under a query none of whose terms reads a message's body (`is:unread`, `tag:`, a `date:` over mail), the store answering such a term whole (search-draft-11; until then a `bodiless` item counted against every query, so a headers-only mailbox never answered complete). What the terms read is `PimdirQuery::reads` (`PimdirSearchReads`), wherever each stands, negated or not: a text term every kind's body, the value of a role field or of `with:` holding `@` being an address, a seek of the store reading none; `date:` and `when:` a calendar item's; `has:attachment` a calendar item's, a message's mark being the store's; `thread:` a message's, one holding no body naming its parent by its `In-Reply-To` alone where its body may name one by its `References`; `has:phone`, `has:address`, `has:birthday`, `has:photo` and `category:` a card's; `is:encrypted` a message's; `content`, set by free text, `body:`, `attachment:` and `mimetype:`; and `mark`, set by `has:attachment`, under which mail's `bodiless` items count though the term reads no message's body, the mark of a message holding none being a guess its body corrects (STORAGE Annex A.1). `PimdirSearchReads::counts_bodiless(kind)` SHALL answer the whole rule for a `bodiless` item of a kind, a term reading a body of the kind or, for mail, the mark, so `is_complete`, the CLI's text and a consumer saying why an answer is partial ask it alone and none restates it; joined by `or` with `kinds.contains(kind)`, as a consumer wrote it while it answered the mark's half, it answers the same (src/search/coverage.rs `a_bodiless_item_counts_under_a_read_of_its_kind_or_mails_mark`, which fails with the mark's half alone). The CLI prints `complete`, `local.kinds`, `local.reads` (`kinds`, `content`, `mark`), `zoneDiffers` and the rest in JSON, and in text the counts the query's terms read alone, a `bodiless` one under a term reading the kind or, for mail, under `has:attachment`, the zone under a complete coverage too.

- THEN the first seven are complete and the last eight are not, the bodiless message counted in both and a hit of `is:unread` (tests/search.rs `a_message_held_without_its_body_counts_only_against_what_reads_it`; the vector cases `collection:home/INBOX is:flagged`, `is:unread`, `subject:reunion`, `has:attachment` and `thread:multi-1@example.org`; src/search/coverage.rs `a_bodiless_message_counts_against_its_attachment_mark`; src/cli/search.rs `a_coverage_prints_the_counts_its_query_reads`; tests/search_cli.rs `a_search_prints_the_four_counts_and_whether_it_is_complete`). Each fails with a bodiless item counted against every query, with `thread:` reading no body or with the mark ignored

#### Scenario: A group read through
- `(collection:home/Kept) is:read` and `((collection:home/Kept) (is:read))` with the coverage and the answer of the bare query, `-(collection:home/Kept) is:flagged` with the whole store's coverage (the vector cases; tests/search.rs `a_coverage_counts_what_the_index_could_not_read`), each failing with a group left unread

### Requirement: Hits are addressable and typed
A hit SHALL be `(account, seq)` with its placements, its kind and title, what it matched (`body`, `summary`, or none when only structured predicates matched) and a snippet cut from its chunks. What it matched SHALL be probed for a page's hits alone (SEARCH §8.2): each positive text term against a body a placement holds (`match_object`), else against the summary of one indexed bodiless (`match_summary`). The CLI's `--json` output SHALL use camelCase keys and publish its JSON Schema (`pimdir-index`, `pimdir-search` for hits and threads alike, `pimdir-count`, `pimdir-show`, `pimdir-thread`, `pimdir-tag`), a refused query failing with `query-refused`, and `show SEQ --account ACCOUNT` SHALL accept a hit's address as is.

### Requirement: The vectors are the bar
tests/search.rs SHALL run every case of pimdir vectors/search, vendored under spec/vectors/search (store.json at `v` 2, queries.json at `v` 4, its 220 cases and its occurrence windows, and chunks.json), against a store built as an owner leaves it, the blob of each item stating `unreadable` then taken out of the blob directory, indexed at the vectors' `now` in `UTC`, then again in each zone a case states, or in its `index_zone` where it names one, for those cases, asked through `open_query` stating the case's `zone`. Every case compares its hits, the four counts of its coverage against those derived from the store file (and against the case's own `coverage` where it states one), its count's coverage, a lag of zero, `horizon_left`, `zone_differs`, and `temporal`, `cut` and `complete` where a case states them; the file's `coverage` is compared with what the index answers over every collection. Every case SHALL run: none is skipped, `threads` cases included, and tests/pending_vectors.txt lists none.

### Requirement: Threads are relinked by the refresh and listed on request
The refresh SHALL relink the messages a batch touched (SEARCH §9): the parent from the held body's first `In-Reply-To` id, else its last `References` id, each field read from its first occurrence (SEARCH §6, §9; src/search/thread.rs `a_parent_is_read_from_the_first_occurrence`; the five vector cases over item 33, mail-repeated-fields.eml), or from `mail_summary.in_reply_to` for a bodiless item; a `message` row for every parent named, present or not; a cycle broken by ignoring the edge that would close it; the thread id the present member with the lowest `(sort_key, link_id)`, its new row written and members repointed before an unused row goes (`delete_thread`). A message no placement holds any more prunes its thread first (below). `thread:` SHALL accept the thread id or any message id in it, so an id renamed by a join still resolves. `PimdirIndex::threads` (CLI `search --threads`) SHALL group hits by account and thread, in the order of each thread's first hit, `count`, `first` and `last` covering the thread's live members in that account; an item that is not mail stands alone.

### Requirement: Dates resolve against a clock in a zone
A query's dates SHALL resolve against `PimdirSearchClock`: `now`, the system clock unless one is given, and `zone`, an IANA name, UTC when `None`, as SEARCH §8.1 states. A year, a month, a day, `today` and `yesterday` SHALL run from the first instant of their first day in the zone to the first instant of the day after their last (`compile::bounds`, through jiff), so a day a transition moves lasts 23 or 25 hours and one whose midnight a transition skips starts after the gap; the temporal coverage's `since` and `until` and `cut_before` read the same bounds. A relative amount SHALL stay an instant: alone it spans from its instant to now, as a range side it is its instant. A bound no instant of a four-digit year states SHALL be open (SEARCH §8.1): a start before `0000-01-01T00:00:00Z`, which a relative amount reaches, and an end past `9999-12-31T23:59:59Z`, which the year 9999, its last month and its last day reach, in the hits, the temporal coverage, the horizon and `cut_before` alike; an end before year 0 is its first instant, the range holding nothing (`compile::bounds`, `a_bound_no_four_digit_year_states_is_open`; a start was clamped to year 0 and an end written with a five-digit year before). A zone the IANA database does not name SHALL be refused as `PimdirError::IndexZone` (`index-zone-unknown`) by `search`, `threads`, `count` and `tag`. `PimdirIndex::floating_zone` SHALL answer the zone the index records (`index_meta.floating_zone`), on an `open_query` handle too, the one a client should state. The CLI's `search`, `count` and `tag` SHALL take `--zone ZONE`, the index's floating zone by default.

### Requirement: Tagging stages through the queue, each placement as its own source
`PimdirIndex::tag` SHALL plan a flag change over a query's hits without writing: per placement its next flag set, starting from the last pending `set-flags` of that placement so tags staged in a row compose, `read`, `flagged`, `answered` and `draft` naming their system flags, `unread` and `encrypted` refused. ### Requirement: The index refreshes without I/O
The crate SHALL offer the index's refresh as a coroutine, `search::refresh::PimdirIndexRefresh::new(now, batch)`, folding one batch of the feed per run (SEARCH §4) and yielding typed reads and typed writes, never SQL, so an owner holding its own SQLite indexes without the crate opening a file. Its public fields state the floating zone (`floating_zone`, `UTC` by default) and whether the run begins a pass and so first folds the placements recorded missing (`missing`, true by default). Its reads are `WantsIndexState` (`index_meta` with the store's change cursor), `WantsReexpand` (`items_to_reexpand`, with the horizons, the time zone database version and the floating zone), `WantsMissing` (`load_missing_items`), `WantsFeed { since, limit }`, `WantsHeld` (the index's placement rows and object hashes among those asked), `WantsBodies { hashes }` (at most `BODIES_MAX`), `WantsSummaries`, `WantsMessages` (a message id's row, children, presence, held body and `In-Reply-To`), `WantsThreadRows` (`thread_rows`, the rows a prune left) and `WantsGone`; its writes are `Writes(Vec<PimdirIndexOp>)`. A run is one transaction: a consumer answers every yield of it inside one write transaction, applies each `Writes` in order as it comes, every read after it seeing it, and commits when the run completes with `PimdirIndexRefreshOutput`: the cursor it recorded and whether the feed was exhausted, so a caller runs again or stops within its deadline. `PimdirIndex::refresh` SHALL be that coroutine answered through the canonical statements, so the reference implementation and an app run one fold.

Each `PimdirIndexOp` SHALL name SEARCH §3's rows, so a consumer of another shape maps it to its statements: `PutObject` (the `object` row, its card categories, its text and its chunks) and `DropObject`, `PutPlacement` and `DropPlacement` (its summary text, flags, occurrences, cut and missing record with it), `PutSummaryText` and `DropSummaryText`, `ReplaceFlags`, `ReplaceOccurrences` (with the item's `cut`, `set_cut`), `PutMissing` and `DropMissing` (`insert_missing`, `delete_missing`; a placement recorded is indexed bodiless, or is a calendar item whose indexed body an expansion could not read), `PutMessage`, `PruneThread` (`unparent_messages` then `prune_messages`), `PutThread` and `DropThread` (`delete_thread`, when no message points to it), `SetCursor`, `SetHorizon`, `SetTzdbVersion` and `SetFloatingZone`. A relink's ops SHALL write the thread rows before the message rows pointing to them, and drop the stale thread rows last.

`WantsFeed` and `WantsMissing` SHALL be answered in the crate's own types, never as the canonical statements' rows (`PimdirIndexFeedRow`: collection, seq, link id, object hash, flag set, deleted, retained, kind, account, stamp, and `PimdirIndexSummary`, the summary row with its lists and its address rows, on every live row holding no body and on every file), so a consumer of another shape maps its own rows into them. A purge or a collection rename SHALL be its own yield, `WantsGone`, asked once by the run exhausting the feed when the store's purge count moved, answered as `placements_gone` names them (`PimdirIndexGonePlacement`, with the link id) and `objects_gone`: the feed pages what the store holds by stamp, and a purged item, a collected object or a renamed collection's old id leaves no row and draws no stamp.

### Requirement: Occurrences are bounded and a cut is reported
An item SHALL write at most 10,000 occurrences (`search::occurrence::OCCURRENCES_MAX`), the first by start then end of those overlapping the horizon, and SHALL examine at most 1,000,000 periods of a rule (`PERIODS_MAX`), a period being `INTERVAL` times `FREQ` from the one holding `DTSTART`, a period before the horizon counting (SEARCH §7). A rule whose first unexamined period starts before the horizon's end, and which neither its `UNTIL` nor its `COUNT` ends before it, is stopped there, its occurrences from that instant on not written. An item either bound stops SHALL record its cut with its occurrences (`set_cut`): the earlier of its 10,001st occurrence's start and its first unexamined period's, `NULL` otherwise. A dated answer's coverage SHALL count the items cut before its range's end (`cut_before`). Departure: the walk is ical-rs's, which skips ahead over periods a `BYxxx` part rules out; the period bound is computed from the rule's `DTSTART`, `FREQ` and `INTERVAL` rather than counted, and the 10,001 first occurrences of each timeline are taken in identity order before the item's are ordered, so an override moving an instance from past them to before them is left out.

### Requirement: A body that cannot be read is tried again
A placement whose body the blob directory does not hold, or whose bytes fail its name, SHALL be indexed bodiless, its summary text written, and recorded in `missing` (`insert_missing`); the first run of every pass SHALL fold the recorded placements again (`load_missing_items`), whether or not the store stamped them again, and one indexed or holding no body leaves the record (`delete_missing`), one deleted taking it by cascade (SEARCH §4).

A calendar row whose expansion read its body has its occurrences replaced and leaves the record; one whose expansion could not read it stays recorded, keeping its occurrences when the index holds that same body and losing those of another body otherwise; and a feed row of a calendar item the page did not expand neither records nor forgets it, since it may be recorded for an expansion it still owes. ### Requirement: The walkers of a message agree
The crate reads a message's parts three ways: the whole-body derivation, the streamed walk (`PimdirAttachmentWalk`, the summaries') and mime-rs's tree (the extraction's). The walkers are not merged, the streamed one carrying bounds Annex A.1 does not have. tests/walk_agrees.rs SHALL hold them together: the streamed walk against the derivation on the mark and the invitation, and, under `search`, against mime-rs's tree on the parts that make the attachment mark, by section and media type, over the same generator, whole and cut anywhere (`tree::a_built_message_has_the_attachments_its_tree_has`), and over the same generator with every entity stating its `Content-Type`, its `Content-Disposition` and its `Content-Transfer-Encoding` twice, the second occurrence saying otherwise, now after the one drawn, now before it (`a_built_message_stating_its_fields_twice_reads_alike`, `tree::a_built_message_stating_its_fields_twice_has_the_attachments_its_tree_has`).

Storage-draft-11 states the two cases the walkers parted on (Annex A.1; SEARCH §6). The last child of a multipart no closing delimiter ends, ended by an outer delimiter or by the end of the message, is a part of all three (summaries.md; mime-rs read it so already). An entity's fields are read from their first occurrence by all three: the summaries' two walks read them so, and mime-rs's tree is the one of the message as `summary::mail::first_occurrences` hands it, the test reading the tree as the extraction does. The standard's two fixtures, mail-unclosed-multipart.eml and mail-repeated-part-fields.eml, are held by the three walkers: the summary cases (tests/summaries.rs), `tree::the_tree_and_the_walk_agree_on_the_fixtures_of_annex_a1`, and tests/search_pure.rs `the_extraction_walks_the_parts_the_summary_walks`, the extraction's own text and attachment field.

- The derivation dropping the last child fails the summary case, the invitation table, the fixture test and both properties. The walk counting the line break the message ends with fails the fixture's size, 8 for 6. The tree read off the message as written fails the fixture test (`[]` for part 2 `text/csv`) and the property over restated fields. The extraction parsing the message as written indexes the repeated-fields fixture whole, its delimiters and its header lines, as one text part.
